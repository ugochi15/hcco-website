About HCCO

Our story, our mission, and the people behind the work.

Our Story

Where it began

Heaven Children Care Organization (HCCO) began with a simple conviction: no child should go to bed hungry or feel forgotten.

Several years before HCCO was formally incorporated, its founders began supporting vulnerable children through small, practical acts of care — particularly by providing meals and assistance through local church communities. What began as a personal effort gradually revealed a much greater need: children and families facing food insecurity and limited access to essential support.

As the outreach grew, so did the vision. HCCO was formally established to create a sustainable organization capable of reaching more children, building meaningful community partnerships, and providing support that goes beyond immediate needs to help children thrive.

In February 2026, Heaven Children Care Organization was formally registered with Nigeria's Corporate Affairs Commission — marking a new chapter in a mission that had already been underway for several years.

Mission

Why we exist

To nourish, support, and empower vulnerable children and families through food assistance, education, care, and community partnerships.

Vision

Where we're headed

A world where every child, regardless of circumstance, has the nourishment, care, opportunity, and support needed to thrive and reach their God-given potential. HCCO envisions growing from its roots in Nigeria into a trusted organization creating lasting impact for vulnerable children and communities wherever the need exists.

Core Values

What guides how we work

Compassion

We see every child as worthy of love, dignity, care, and the opportunity to thrive.

Faith

Our Christian faith inspires us to serve selflessly, love practically, and remain hopeful even in difficult circumstances.

Integrity

We are committed to honesty, accountability, responsible stewardship, and transparency in everything entrusted to us.

Empowerment

We seek not only to meet immediate needs, but to create opportunities that help children and communities build stronger futures.

Our Faith

What guides us

HCCO is a Christian faith-rooted organization, inspired by the example of Jesus Christ and His call to love and care for others, especially those who are vulnerable.

We believe every child is created by God with inherent worth, dignity, and purpose. Our faith calls us to put compassion into action — feeding the hungry, caring for those in need, and serving without discrimination. While our Christian faith guides who we are, our care is extended to children and families regardless of their faith, background, ethnicity, or circumstance.
