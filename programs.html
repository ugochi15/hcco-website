Our Programs

Practical, consistent care across four areas of need.

Active

Food & Nutrition

Providing meals and nutritional support to children and families facing food insecurity, helping ensure that basic hunger is not a barrier to a child's health and growth.

So far, HCCO has reached over 500 children through this program, delivered in partnership with 7 churches and communities.

Planned

Education & Child Development

Supporting learning and healthy childhood development, so children have the tools and encouragement to grow beyond their immediate circumstances.

Planned

Care & Wellbeing

Holistic care for the wellbeing of the children and families HCCO serves — addressing needs beyond food and education alone.

Active

Community & Church Partnerships

Working together with churches and local communities to extend HCCO's reach and ground the work in trusted, existing relationships.

HCCO currently partners with 7 churches and communities, supported by approximately 20 active volunteers.
